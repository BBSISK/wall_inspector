Conservation Engineering Protocol (BS 8221 & Eurocode 6)

All specified pointing mortars must utilize naturally hydraulic lime (NHL 2 or NHL 3.5 conforming to EN 459-1) matched to the compressive strength and vapor permeability of the host masonry. Under no circumstances should impermeable Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC) or polymer sealants be applied to historic bedding planes. Stainless helical crack stitches must be austenitic grade 316 (1.4401) grouted with shrink-compensated thixotropic grout.
