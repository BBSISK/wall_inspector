Target: Salt Leaching

Crystalline Salt Leaching (Efflorescence)

Moderate Risk
Context & Pathology:

Late 19th-century Manchester residential facade. Internal moisture evaporates from rain-saturated clay wythes, depositing white sodium and calcium sulfate crystals on the external skin.

Failure Mechanics:
