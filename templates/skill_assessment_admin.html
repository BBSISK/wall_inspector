Photographic Capture Standards & Ingestion Protocol

Calibrated Ingestion Spec

1. Lighting & Illumination

  • Diffuse Overcast Daylight: Recommended 5000K–6500K overcast sky to eliminate harsh shadow masking and specular hot spots.
  • Controlled Raking Light: Use 15°–30° shallow oblique sun ONLY when recording face bulges, delamination blisters, or contour scaling.
  • Avoid Direct Midday Sun: Prevents deep shadows inside open bed and perpend joints.

2. Orthogonal Normal Angle

  • Strict 90° Normal Angle: Position optical axis perpendicular to wall face plane.
  • 0° Pitch & 0° Roll Tilt: Eliminates trapezoidal keystoning, perspective tilt, and dimensional compression.
  • Sensor Parallel: Maintain sensor plane strictly parallel to continuous bed courses.

3. Scope, Coursing & Scale

  • 3 to 5 Courses: Frame must capture 3 to 5 continuous horizontal courses to establish masonry bond rhythm.
  • 1.5m to 2.5m Width: Provides adequate structural context across the wythe.
  • Visible Datum & Scale: Include plinth base/coping or place a 1m survey rule/scale coin at frame margin.

4. Resolution & Depth of Field

  • Minimum 1080p (2MP+): Native 4K / 8MP+ recommended for zoomable arris inspection.
  • Arris Focus Lock: Lock autofocus sharply on bed and perpend joint arrises.
  • Aperture f/5.6 – f/8: Deep depth of field ensures both proud and recessed stones remain in razor focus.
